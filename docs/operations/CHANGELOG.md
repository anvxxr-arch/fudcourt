| **Portfolio risk gates are enforced, not advisory** — `maxOpenRiskPct` (§73) and `maxDailyLossPct` (§74) refuse NEW OPENINGS before the row exists, so a refusal leaves nothing to reconcile; open risk aggregates in one SQL statement (`SUM(COALESCE(current_risk, planned_risk))` over live executions) so the ceiling is checked against a consistent snapshot, and current risk from real fills wins over the stale plan. | **Chase Limit and TWAP jitter actually work** — an audit against PRD §128 found the order-MANAGING strategies were untested and broken. `placeEntry` bailed on `remaining <= 0`, which a working child order always produces, so **chase never repriced at all** (it emitted `complete` on the tick after its first placement); the reprice placed its replacement **without cancelling the old one**, leaving two live orders on one position (§107 violation); the replacement budget was a constant assigned on both ternary branches and counted the first placement, so `maxReplacements: 1` gave zero repricings and `0` refused to trade. `TwapConfig` (§29) was declared but unreachable — both jitters hard-wired to 0 — and the final slice's remainder was float-computed, so **every TWAP finished 1e-8 short** of its planned quantity | `apps/web/src/platform/executor/{engine,plan,types}.ts`, `src/features/executor/ui.tsx` | `executor-engine-tests.ts` **20/20** (6 new): jittered slices still sum to the target, offsets stay monotonic inside the duration, the reprice cancels before replacing, and a spent budget stops the chase — measured `place@99995 → cancel → place@99981 → cancel → place@99967 → stop` with 2/2 replacements used; total suite **222/222** (137 executor tests, incl. 8 new composer-seam tests), tsc 0, build green | DR-021 §2g |
| **The composer→API seam is now tested, and the executor is priced in the inbound limiter** — a control present in the form but dropped from the request is invisible to every other suite, so `buildExecution` is exported as the pure seam and pinned by 8 new tests (blank jitter/budget ⇒ key ABSENT; an explicit `0` replacement budget ⇒ transmitted, since "place once, never reprice" is a real instruction that a truthiness filter would swallow). | **Scale In is priced per level, not off its top level** - §33 says the risk engine must budget *every* potential fill, but a ladder was sized like a single entry at the reference price, so the same budget bought more size than it could pay for; `estimatedEntry` also reported the top-level limit while the position's realized entry is the ladder VWAP, and that one number feeds liquidation, profit projection and the R:R the user reads. Sizing is now exact decimal arithmetic over the levels (`Q = budget / riskPerUnit`, rounded DOWN so the bound survives rounding), fees are charged per level on their own notional, and the §37 hard-bound resize re-solves the ladder instead of calling a single-reference resolver that would return a quantity breaching the bound | `apps/web/src/platform/executor/plan.ts`, `scripts/tests/executor-plan-tests.ts`, `scripts/verify/executor-paper-e2e.ts` | `executor-plan-tests.ts` **25/25** (7 new); total offline suite **228/228** (143 executor tests); paper E2E **42/42** vs real Postgres - the ladder buys 0.0132 on the same $40 that buys 0.0198 for a single entry, and reports entry 96,000 | DR-021 §2i |
